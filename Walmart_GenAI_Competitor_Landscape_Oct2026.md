# Walmart vs. Top 5 Competitors: GenAI and Agentic AI Competitor Landscape

**Prepared for:** Competitor Landscape Lead, Team Retail. "Grounded in the Customer — Retail" exercise, Architect Transformation Program (Toronto, 26–30 October 2026)
**Customer:** Walmart
**Competitors covered:** Amazon, Target, Costco, Kroger, Alibaba Group
**Research date:** 28 October 2026
**Guiding question:** How are retail leaders using GenAI for product content, customer service automation, store operations and inventory allocation, and where does Walmart stand?

## 1. Executive summary

- **Walmart is a strong No. 2 in retail GenAI.** It trails only Amazon among US retailers.
  - It leads on AI for store associates.
  - It is close to Amazon on its shopping assistant and on delivery speed.
- **It is behind in two places:**
  - **Selling through outside AI assistants** (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot). Target leads here, and Walmart shut down its ChatGPT Instant Checkout.
  - **Publishing results for returns and customer-service AI.**
- **Amazon is the scale leader, with a closed model.**
  - Its shopping assistant (formerly Rufus, now Alexa for Shopping) has 350M+ users and added about $12B in sales in 2025 (company claim).
  - It blocks outside AI shopping agents, and lost an appeals ruling against Perplexity in August 2026.
- **Target is the fastest mover on selling inside outside AI assistants.**
  - You can buy from Target inside ChatGPT, Google Gemini/AI Mode and Microsoft Copilot, with its Circle loyalty program linked.
  - Traffic from AI platforms rose 2,000% year over year in Q1 2026.
  - Target appointed its first Chief AI Officer in August 2026.
- **Costco is cautious but shows clear payback.**
  - AI pharmacy reordering keeps in-stocks above 98%.
  - Personalized product carousels drove about $470M in online sales in one quarter.
  - Its CFO says Costco is not pursuing agentic commerce "at the moment".
- **Kroger is running Walmart's own playbook under former Walmart executive Greg Foran.**
  - Its AI shopping assistant is live on all banners, and repeat users spend 11% more.
  - It wrote off about $2.5B after closing three Ocado robotic warehouses.
- **Alibaba is the most advanced example of AI shopping assistants doing the buying.**
  - Its Qwen app can shop across 4B+ Taobao/Tmall products, and 250M users have tried it.
  - The payback is not yet visible: quarterly profit fell 75% and merchant ad revenue fell 7%.

## 3. Where Walmart stands on each business pressure

### P1 — Inventory accuracy and allocation: at parity, but needs proof of accuracy
- **Who sets the bar:**
  - **Target:** physical audits found that half its out-of-stocks were invisible to its systems. Machine-learning models now spot this "phantom" inventory, reset counts automatically and trigger restocking.
  - **Amazon:** its foundation forecasting model improved national forecasts for deal events by 10% and regional forecasts by 20%.
  - **Freshippo/Hema (Alibaba's grocery chain):** reports 25% fewer stockouts and 30% less waste (company claim).
  - **Kroger:** its bakery RFID pilot cut waste by 50%.
- **Walmart:**
  - AI agents choose the best store or warehouse to fill each order.
  - More than 1M handheld scanners use computer vision to map inventory.
  - Inventory grew 6% in Q2 FY27, slightly faster than sales. In Q4 FY26 it had grown at about half the rate of sales.

### P2 — Returns, fraud and margin: gap on returns AI, strength on profit mix
- **Who sets the bar:**
  - **Alibaba:** runs a model that detects AI-faked defect photos in refund disputes.
  - **Amazon:** uses machine learning to find refund-fraud rings.
  - **Target:** co-leads the NRF retail fraud taxonomy, which now covers returns and refund abuse.
- **Walmart:**
  - It has published no figures on returns AI.
  - Its terms for AI shopping agents say Walmart's normal returns policy applies even if an agent makes a mistake.
  - Profit is shifting toward advertising: Walmart Connect grew 43% in Q2 FY27, and FY26 global ad revenue was $6.4B.

### P3 — Omnichannel experience and loyalty: challenger to Amazon, behind Target on outside AI assistants
- **Amazon:**
  - Its assistant has 350M+ users and added about $12B in sales in 2025.
  - Users spend 40%+ more per order.
  - People who try Alexa+ sign up for Prime at nearly 25% higher rates.
- **Target:** sells inside three outside AI platforms with loyalty linked. AI traffic is growing more than 3.5 times faster than the industry.
- **Kroger:** repeat users of its assistant spend 11% more.
- **Costco:** personalization drove about $470M in online sales in one quarter.
- **Walmart:**
  - Sparky has 70% more users than a year ago, and those users spend 40% more per order.
  - ChatGPT Instant Checkout converted at about one-third the rate of Walmart.com and was replaced.
  - A gateway for outside AI shopping agents went into beta on 21 October 2026.

### P4 — Supply chain speed and resilience: co-leader with Amazon
- **Amazon:**
  - Delivered 40%+ more items same day or overnight in the first half of 2026.
  - Same-day fresh grocery reaches 2,300+ towns.
- **Walmart:**
  - Stores fill 80% of online orders and 100% of fast deliveries.
  - About 60% of the US population can get a delivery in 30 minutes or less.
  - More than 50% of online-order volume runs through automated facilities.
- **Kroger:** its retreat from Ocado (about $2.5B in charges) supports Walmart's store-based model.

### P5 — Labor scheduling and store execution: Walmart leads
- **Walmart:**
  - AI tools reach 1.5M associates.
  - Shift planning dropped from 90 minutes to 30.
  - Associates' use of tech tools helped keep wage costs growing slower than sales in Q2 FY27.
- **Competitors:**
  - **Kroger:** its associate assistant, Sage, has up to 150,000 users.
  - **Target:** its Store Companion runs in about 2,000 stores, with no results published.
  - **Costco:** pre-scan checkout is up to 20% faster.
  - **Amazon:** retired its Blue Jay warehouse robot.

## 4. Walmart (the customer): detailed profile

### 4.1 Strategy, owners, build vs. buy, investment and workforce
- **CEO transition.**
  - John Furner became President and CEO on 1 February 2026, succeeding Doug McMillon, who retired on 31 January 2026.
  - Furner describes Walmart as becoming "AI native".
- **The four "super agents"** were introduced in July 2025 by CTO Suresh Kumar:
  - **Sparky:** the customer shopping agent, live in the Walmart app.
  - **Associate agent:** brings schedules, sales data and tasks into one place.
  - **Marty:** helps suppliers, sellers and advertisers with onboarding, orders and campaigns.
  - **Developer agent:** speeds up building, testing and launching.
- **Executive owners:**
  - **Suresh Kumar:** EVP, Global CTO and Chief Development Officer.
  - **Daniel Danker:** EVP, AI Acceleration, Product and Design. He joined from Instacart in July 2025.
  - **David Guggina:** CEO, Walmart U.S.
  - **Seth Dallaire:** Chief Growth Officer, overseeing Walmart Connect and Walmart+.
  - Furner's reorganization centralized Walmart's technology platforms.
- **Build and partner.**
  - Walmart's AI tools run on Element, its own machine-learning platform.
  - Sparky was built in-house and can use different models: open-source models combined with retail-specific models trained on Walmart data.
  - CFO John David Rainey says Walmart is developing AI through partnerships.
  - **OpenAI:** ChatGPT Enterprise rollout and OpenAI certifications for associates.
  - **Google:** Gemini shopping integration, announced at NRF in January 2026.
  - **Meta:** shopping inside its Muse AI assistant.
  - *Unverified/Assumption:* the status of Walmart's retail-specific "Wallaby" models was not re-confirmed.
- **Investment.**
  - Capital spending is about 4% of net sales, and AI spending is included in it.
  - No separate AI dollar figure has been disclosed.
  - Spending on supply-chain automation is described as peaking.
- **Workforce.**
  - Walmart plans to hold its 2.1M global headcount flat for three years.
  - About 1,000 tech and product roles were cut or relocated in May 2026; a source said this was not driven by AI automation.
  - That followed about 1,500 corporate cuts in May 2025.
  - The 10-K refers to using AI to automate repetitive tasks.

### 4.2 Product content, search and discovery (P3, P2)
- **Catalog enrichment (at scale).**
  - LLMs created or improved more than 850M catalog data points, work Walmart said would have needed about 100 times its headcount (August 2024).
  - The pipeline uses one model as a "writer" and a second as an "editor".
  - Values are accepted only above attribute-specific accuracy thresholds, typically 90–95%+.
- **Sparky (at scale in the US, expanding).**
  - **Q4 FY26:** about half of app users had tried it.
  - **Q1 FY27:** weekly active users more than doubled in one quarter, and units bought through Sparky grew more than 4x. It added in-store use, auto-reorder and Spanish.
  - **Q2 FY27:** users up 70% year over year, spending 40% more per order. The uplift was reported as about 35% earlier in 2026.
- **Selling through outside AI platforms (scaling; early economics).**
  - Sparky now runs inside Meta Muse, ChatGPT and Gemini.
  - The Walmart Agent Gateway, built on the MCP protocol, launched as a beta on 21 October 2026.
  - "Agent Profiles", launching about a month later, will let customers set spending limits for AI agents.
- **Retail media and partner tools (beta to scaling).**
  - An AI ad assistant for Sponsored Search is in beta; 97% of the questions advertisers ask it are unique.
  - Ad formats inside Sparky are being tested.
  - Walmart acquired Vibe to add self-service tools for advertisers.
  - Trend-to-Product compresses fashion production timelines by up to 18 weeks.
  - AI listing tools for Marketplace sellers launched in August 2025.

### 4.3 Customer service automation (P3, P2)
- **Customer Support Assistant (in production).** It routes inquiries, resolves common issues and increasingly handles tasks end to end.
- **Outcome.** It cut resolution times by up to 40% (October 2025). This is a single company-reported figure with no baseline.
- **Roadmap.** More advanced customer-care agents, and support built into Sparky.
- **Controls for outside AI agents:**
  - Walmart's standard returns process applies even if an agent does not act as intended.
  - Agents never receive customers' card details.
  - Agents that break the terms are blocked.
- **Gap (Unverified/Assumption):** no returns-specific GenAI metrics and no contact-center containment figures were found.

### 4.4 Store operations (P5, P1, P2)
- **Associate tools (scaled).**
  - Real-time translation and AI task management rolled out in June 2025, cutting shift planning from 90 minutes to 30.
  - The task tool started with overnight stocking. A secondary source says it is expanding to other shifts.
  - The associate conversational assistant is getting a GenAI upgrade.
  - More than 1M US associates carry handhelds that use computer vision to map inventory.
  - Associates' use of tech tools contributed to wage leverage in Q2 FY27.
- **Inventory tools and facilities.**
  - VizPick (augmented reality) is being paired with RFID for apparel in select stores (pilot).
  - Digital twins of stores predict equipment failures up to two weeks ahead, and emergency alerts are down more than 30%.
  - Drones cut site-scoping time by 75%.
- **Sam's Club exit technology.**
  - AI exit arches let members leave 23% faster; they were live in 120+ clubs as of April 2024.
  - The 10-K describes "Just Go" as a friction-free exit experience.
  - Sam's Club's remodel program removes traditional checkouts.
  - Third-party claims of a full-fleet rollout are unverified.
- **Shrink.** Not quantified in FY26–FY27 disclosures (Unverified).

### 4.5 Inventory allocation and supply chain (P1, P4)
- **Allocation AI (at scale).**
  - Agentic workflows help anticipate demand and decide where inventory sits.
  - The Fulfillment Engine uses AI agents to choose the best store or fulfillment center for each order.
  - Digital twins of the logistics network stress-test disruptions.
- **Inventory outcomes are mixed.**
  - **Q4 FY26:** inventory grew 2.6%, about half the rate of sales, with fewer fresh-food write-offs and markdowns.
  - **Q2 FY27:** inventory grew 6% in constant currency, slightly faster than sales. Walmart attributes this to cost inflation and inventory built for strategic initiatives.
- **Automation (roughly halfway).**
  - About 3,100 US stores receive some automated freight.
  - More than 50% of online fulfillment volume runs through automated facilities.
  - Symbotic bought Walmart's robotics unit in January 2025, and Walmart is paying $520M toward the development program.
  - The first in-store SymMicro system is about six months away. Symbotic's CEO has signaled the design will change before any wide rollout (pilot).
- **Delivery speed.**
  - Fast deliveries in the US grew 48% in Q2 FY27, and sub-30-minute delivery covers 38 markets.
  - About 60% of the US population can be reached in 30 minutes or less.
  - Drones had 66 live locations and 1M lifetime deliveries as of May 2026, against a January 2026 plan of 270 sites.
- **Data for suppliers.** The Scintilla data platform is being extended to Sam's Club U.S.
  - *Unverified/Assumption:* no current evidence was found of GenAI for supplier negotiations or of the Eden fresh-food system.

| Customer service | 3 | Assistant in production with a 40% resolution-time claim; little else measured; no returns AI disclosed |

### 4.7 Walmart's top five business pressures (plain language)
1. **Stretched shoppers and price investment (P3, P2).** Customers are still under pressure, and Walmart expects more than $2B in extra fuel-related costs this year.
2. **Tariffs and cost swings (P4, P2).** Walmart received about $2.9B in tariff refunds and put the money into lower prices. New tariff rules remain uncertain.
3. **Profit mix and online economics (P2).** Growth depends on ads, membership and marketplace, which are about one-third of operating income.
4. **Health-care pricing rules (P2).** New Medicare drug-pricing rules ("Maximum Fair Price") reduced comparable sales by 125 basis points.
5. **Labor productivity and competition (P5, P3).** Headcount is flat while the business grows, and higher self-insurance costs offset some savings. The 10-K warns of new business models and well-funded new competitors.

### 4.8 Walmart's competitive position (facts)
- Stores are the last-mile fulfillment point for 80% of online orders and 100% of fast deliveries, across 4,611 US stores.
- Online sales are more than 23% of Walmart U.S. sales, with 10 straight quarters of 20%+ growth.
- US online sales earned double-digit incremental margins in the first half of the year.
- Walmart Connect grew 43% in Q2 FY27, and FY26 global ad revenue was $6.4B (+46%). The CFO acknowledges ad penetration still trails "best-in-class competitors" (implicitly Amazon).
- Members spend about four times more than non-members.
- Walmart became the first retailer to pass $1T in market value.
- Walmart is open to outside AI agents through open protocols; Amazon blocks them. This contrast comes from vendor commentary (low–moderate confidence).

### 4.9 Setbacks and risks
- **Instant Checkout dropped.** About 200,000 products were listed in ChatGPT, but purchases made inside the chatbot converted at about one-third the rate. Walmart replaced it with Sparky-led experiences that keep checkout on Walmart's side. Reports that conversion later recovered to about 70% of Walmart.com come from secondary sources only.
- **Partner dependence.** OpenAI changed its own priorities toward product research, and partner roadmaps are outside Walmart's control.
- **Accuracy.** Walmart's own Sparky help page warns that mistakes can happen.
- **Too many agents.** Kumar acknowledged that several agents, even if each is useful, can confuse users.
- **Workforce and reputation.** The headcount freeze, repeated corporate cuts and the "AI will change every job" message create workforce-relations risk.
- **Pricing trust.** Walmart's public line that it prices products, not people, suggests it is pre-empting concerns about personalized pricing (Assumption).
- **Store robotics timing.** The SymMicro redesign adds timing risk.

## 5. Amazon: detailed profile

### 5.1 Summary
- **AI is a company-wide bet.**
  - CEO Andy Jassy describes AI as a multiplier, not a standalone initiative.
  - 2026 capital spending is about $220B, raised from about $200B partly because of memory costs.
  - Most of that goes to AWS and AI, not to retail.
- **The shopping assistant is the flagship.**
  - Rufus and Alexa+ were merged into "Alexa for Shopping" on 13 May 2026.
  - It has 350M+ users in 12 months and added about $12B in sales in 2025 (company claim).
- **Amazon blocks outside AI agents.** It has blocked dozens of them, but a US appeals court overturned the ruling that had barred Perplexity's shopping agent (4 August 2026).
- **Supply-chain AI is its deepest advantage.** Same-day and overnight items rose 40%+ in the first half of 2026, and same-day fresh grocery reaches 2,300+ towns.
- **Pulling back from its own stores.** All 57 Amazon Fresh and 15 Amazon Go stores are closing, Amazon One palm payment is being dropped, and investment is shifting to Whole Foods.
- **Warehouse automation is at scale.** More than 1M robots run in its warehouses, though the Blue Jay robot was retired (February 2026).

### 5.2 Strategy, owners and build vs. buy
- **Owners:**
  - Andy Jassy, CEO.
  - Doug Herrington, CEO of Worldwide Amazon Stores.
  - Rajiv Mehta, VP Conversational Shopping.
  - Peter DeSantis, SVP, who leads Amazon's AGI, chip and quantum work.
  - Udit Madan, SVP Worldwide Operations.
  - Jason Buechel, CEO of Whole Foods, who now oversees worldwide grocery.
- **Build and buy at once:**
  - Rufus was built on Amazon Bedrock using Anthropic's Claude Sonnet, Amazon Nova and a custom model.
  - Amazon is pursuing its own frontier model to control costs.
  - It committed up to $50B to OpenAI (February 2026), including custom OpenAI models for its customer apps.
  - It committed $5B, plus up to $20B more, to Anthropic (April 2026).
  - It cut jobs in its AGI group in July 2026. DeSantis acknowledged Amazon's models have not been at the very frontier.
- **Workforce:**
  - In June 2025 Jassy said AI would shrink the corporate workforce.
  - About 30,000 corporate jobs have been cut since October 2025, nearly 10% of the corporate workforce.
  - Whether AI caused those cuts is unresolved (Assumption).

### 5.3 Product content and discovery (P3, P2)
- **Alexa for Shopping (at scale).**
  - It answers questions in the main search bar, builds buying guides, shows up to a year of price history, buys automatically at a target price and restocks on a schedule.
  - Active users nearly doubled, and interactions rose more than 5x year over year.
  - US users spend 40%+ more per order.
  - People who try Alexa+ sign up for Prime at nearly 25% higher rates.
- **Ads inside the assistant.**
  - Clicks on sponsored prompts convert 48% more often, with 21% higher spend.
  - Advertisers using the AI Ads Agent see 8% lower cost per impression and 6% lower cost per acquisition.
  - Full-Funnel Campaigns create video and display ads from existing product pages.
- **Seller and generated content.**
  - Nearly half a million sellers used GenAI listing tools by mid-2025.
  - AI review highlights get hundreds of millions of impressions each week.
  - Lens visual search is in 21 countries, and Lens Live lifted engagement by 13%.
- **Buy for Me (pilot).** It buys from other brands' websites inside Amazon's app, and its catalog grew from 65K to 500K+ products. Amazon still calls it an "experiment".
- **Stance on outside agents.**
  - Amazon blocks ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, Grok, Google crawlers and Meta Muse.
  - Jassy argues outside agents lack shopping history and often get prices wrong.
  - Amazon also sells its shopping-assistant technology to other retailers through AWS (e.g., Kate Spade).

### 5.4 Customer service automation (P2, P3)
- **Shopper customer service.** Amazon rebuilt its customer-service chatbot with GenAI. No recent figures on how many contacts it resolves.
- **Seller Assistant (formerly Project Amelia; at scale in the US).**
  - Since 23 September 2026 it remembers each seller's business and runs automations while the seller is logged out, such as restocking and pricing changes. It keeps audit trails and follows seller-set limits.
  - Sellers act on its recommendations about 90% of the time.
  - Amazon says it eliminated 37M reasons for sellers to contact support in the past year.
- **Returns and fraud.**
  - Machine-learning models detect refund-fraud rings.
  - Amazon blocked hundreds of millions of suspected fake reviews in 2025.
  - GenAI triage in Amazon's outsourced support for seller-fulfilled orders is reported by one analytics firm only (Unverified).

### 5.5 Store and facility operations (P5, P4)
- **Own stores: pulling back.**
  - All Fresh and Go stores are closing; Amazon says it had not found a distinctive experience with the right economics.
  - Just Walk Out was removed from Amazon's grocery stores in 2024. It now runs in 360+ third-party locations and 40+ fulfillment-center breakrooms.
  - Amazon plans 100+ new Whole Foods stores, the smaller Daily Shop format, a store-within-a-store pilot and an undisclosed "mass" format.
- **Fulfillment centers (at scale).**
  - Amazon expects to more than double its fleet of robotic arms in 2026.
  - DeepFleet, a GenAI foundation model, coordinates robot movement.
  - Project Eluna, an AI assistant for operations managers, is being piloted in Tennessee.
- **Delivery drivers (pilot to scaling).**
  - About 5,000 smart glasses are planned for this year, rising to 20,000+ by the end of 2027.
  - Drivers have made 275,000+ deliveries wearing them.
  - The glasses are optional, and Amazon has published no data on fewer delivery errors.

### 5.6 Inventory allocation and supply chain (P1, P4)
- **Forecasting.**
  - A foundation forecasting model adds weather and holiday data to sales history. It improved long-term national forecasts for deal events by 10% and regional forecasts by 20%, in the US, Canada, Mexico and Brazil.
  - The Supply Chain Optimization Technologies (SCOT) team decides what to buy and where to place it.
  - Amazon now sells this capability externally as AWS Connect Decisions (April 2026).
- **Speed.**
  - 85+ same-day sites carry Amazon's top 90,000 products.
  - Amazon Now (ultra-fast delivery) runs in 9 countries and 250+ cities.
- **Perishables.**
  - Same-day perishables sales have grown more than 40x since launch.
  - Monthly active perishables customers grew 50%+ this year.
  - Grocery is a $150B+ gross-sales business (2025).
- **Resilience.**
  - Amazon forward-bought and pre-positioned inventory to avoid tariff costs.
  - It sells its logistics network to other companies (Amazon Supply Chain Services; e.g., Procter & Gamble, 3M).

### 5.7 Amazon maturity scorecard
| Area | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Product content and discovery | 5 | 350M+ assistant users, about $12B incremental sales, ads already earning money inside the assistant |
| Customer service | 3 | Seller side is agentic and at scale; shopper side has no disclosed metrics |
| Store and facility operations | 4 warehouses / 2 own stores | Robotics at full scale; exiting its own store formats; Blue Jay retired |
| Inventory and supply chain | 5 | Foundation forecasting model in production, record delivery speed, now sold externally |

### 5.8 Setbacks and risks
- **Legal.** The 9th Circuit reversed an injunction against Perplexity. It held that users acting through AI agents, not Perplexity itself, accessed Amazon's site. This is the first federal appeals ruling on AI agents' access to platforms.
- **Hypocrisy accusations.** Brands say Buy for Me listed their products without consent or showed them inaccurately.
- **Accuracy.** Wired found Alexa+ unreliable after a month of testing (March 2026).
- **Labor.**
  - Leaked documents, reported by the New York Times, projected avoiding 600,000+ US hires by 2033; Amazon disputes this reading.
  - There are privacy concerns about the smart glasses (secondary source).
- **Financial.** Free cash flow fell from $38B to $11B in 2025, mostly because of AI spending.
- **Timing.** Amazon reports Q3 2026 results on 29 October 2026, so some figures may change.

### 5.9 Implications for Walmart
- **Ahead: an assistant that already earns money.** Walmart needs equally attributable results for Sparky (our analysis).
- **Ahead: same-day fresh grocery.** This goes straight at Walmart's grocery core.
- **Ahead: becoming sellers' main operating tool.** Amazon's Seller Central now connects sales across Amazon, eBay, Shopify, TikTok and Walmart (Assumption: this risks Amazon becoming the default system for Walmart Marketplace sellers).
- **Vulnerable: its closed stance.** Walmart is open to outside agents, and on Prime Day visitors arriving from AI assistants were 40% more likely to buy.
- **Vulnerable: stores and labor.** Amazon has retreated from its own store formats, while Walmart's store network and workforce remain advantages.

## 6. Target: detailed profile

### 6.1 Summary
- **AI is a turnaround pillar.**
  - "Accelerating technology" is one of four priorities under CEO Michael Fiddelke, who officially took over in February 2026.
  - Target is adding $2B of extra investment in 2026: about $1B of capital spending and $1B of operating expense. Total 2026 capital spending is about $5B; the AI share is not disclosed.
- **AI has a dedicated owner.**
  - Prat Vemana, EVP and Chief Information and Product Officer, owns technology.
  - Chandhu Nair (formerly at Lowe's) became Target's first Chief AI Officer on 24 August 2026 and reports to Vemana.
- **Most differentiated move: selling inside outside AI assistants.**
  - Target says it is the first mass retailer you can shop inside three AI platforms: Google (AI Mode/Gemini, through the Universal Commerce Protocol it co-developed), Microsoft Copilot Checkout and ChatGPT.
  - Baskets can hold multiple items, and Circle loyalty accounts are linked.
- **Sales are recovering, but Target does not credit AI.** Comparable sales fell 2.6% in FY2025 and rose 3.8% in Q2 2026, with digital comps up 8.7%.

### 6.2 Product content and discovery (P3, P2)
- **GenAI product pages (2024).** GenAI summarized reviews and improved descriptions and titles on thousands of pages. A "100,000+ pages" figure appears only in secondary trackers (Unverified).
- **Search rebuild (scaled).** Target moved to keyword-plus-vector search on Google's AlloyDB AI, improving product-discovery relevance by 20% (September 2025). It also published work on LLM-based accessory recommendations (June 2025).
- **Shopping tools.**
  - **Holiday 2025:** a conversational AI Gift Finder, a List Scanner that turns handwritten lists into carts, and an upgraded in-store Store Mode.
  - **Back-to-school 2026:** AI-powered wish lists and a personalized app home screen. Wish-list creation rose 50%+, items added more than doubled, and conversion on key pages rose nearly 20%.
- **Selling inside outside AI assistants (scaled, early volume).**
  - The ChatGPT app launched in beta in November 2025, supporting fresh food, Drive Up, Order Pickup and shipping.
  - Google and Copilot checkout followed by June 2026, with Circle benefits (an extra 5% off with the Target Circle Card).
  - AI-driven traffic rose 2,000% versus about 400% for retail overall (Q1 2026). Management still calls the channel "still small in total".
- **Merchandising and media.**
  - Trend Brain combines visual analysis of fashion photos with social-media sentiment. Collections rotate nearly twice as fast, and markdowns fall.

### 6.3 Customer service automation (P3, P2)

### 6.4 Store operations (P5, P1)

- **Head-office productivity.**

### 6.5 Inventory allocation and supply chain (P1, P4)

  - Category-specific models find phantom inventory, reset on-hand counts to zero and trigger restocking.

## 7. Costco: detailed profile

- **Deliberately practical.** CEO Ron Vachris says AI goes in only where it strengthens the membership model. Costco has made no broad AI pronouncements, and its CFO says it is not yet chasing agentic commerce.
- **Owners:**
  - CEO Ron Vachris.

  - EVP and Chief Information and Digital Officer Javier Polit (since 2023; formerly at P&G, Coca-Cola and Mondelez).
  - No Chief AI Officer found.

  - Google Cloud Vertex AI for AI and data.
  - Microsoft Dynamics 365 for Costco Travel.
  - Accenture with Google Cloud built a Unified Data Platform that cut member-audience creation from weeks to about 30 minutes (vendor claim).
- **Strongest AI results are behind the scenes and focused on member value:**
  - **Pharmacy AI:** in-stocks above 98%.
  - **Personalization:** more than $470M in online sales in one quarter.
- **AI-search tailwind without paid ads.**
  - AI-search traffic grew by triple digits for two quarters and converts best of any source.
  - Costco membership is among the top items people find through AI searches.

### 7.2 Product content and discovery (P3)
- **Personalization (scaled).**
  - **Q2 FY26:** recommendation carousels drove $470M+ in online sales.
  - **Q3 FY26:** carousels converted at about 3x the typical rate and contributed just under $500M. One transcript reads "$5 billion", which is implausible because online is only about 7% of sales.
  - **Q4 FY26:** sales from personalization grew by triple digits, and 10% of costco.com orders include a personalized item.
- **GenAI content for AI assistants (new).** Costco is using AI to improve product pages so they show up well in Gemini, Anthropic and OpenAI tools.
- **No shopping assistant or agentic checkout.** The CFO says AI is mainly a search tool for members for now.
- **Discontinued.** The Costco Next marketplace (September 2026); its popular items are moving into the main app and site.
- **Retail media.** New work with Google Commerce Media and YouTube. GrowthLoop is used, but not its agentic features.
- **Travel.** Travelport AI personalizes trip search on Costco Travel.

### 7.3 Customer service automation (P3, P2)
- **No GenAI member-service chatbot or agent assist found** (Unverified/Assumption).
- **Digital self-service:**
  - Prescription pay-ahead and pickup lockers; many US warehouses now handle a third or more of prescriptions as pay-ahead.
  - A digital membership card.
- **Retention.** Targeted digital messages helped the US/Canada renewal rate reach 92.3% (Q4 FY26).
- **Governance.** According to a third-party summary, the FY2026 10-K (filed 7 October 2026) adds AI-specific risks:
  - Monitoring autonomous systems and possible bypass of internal controls.
  - Data leakage.
  - Inaccurate AI outputs.
  - AI tools misstating Costco's prices or membership value.

### 7.4 Store operations (P5, P3)
- **Front-end technology (scaled; mostly not GenAI).**
  - Membership scanning at entry, a digital wallet, and staff pre-scanning baskets make checkout up to 20% faster.
  - Automated pay stations average about 8 seconds per transaction. After a successful pilot they are going into new and high-volume warehouses, and pre-scan has rolled out internationally.
- **Labor outcomes.**
  - Technology productivity fully offset wage investments and longer hours (Q1 FY26).
  - Front-end tech lowered payroll in those areas (Q3 FY26).
- **Labor scheduling AI.** Appears only in AI-tracker sites (Unverified).
- **Pushback.** Some members and employees find pre-scan slower.

### 7.5 Inventory allocation and supply chain (P1, P4, P2)
- **Pharmacy (scaled, proven).** AI compares vendor drug prices and reorders automatically, with:
  - In-stocks above 98%.
  - Mid-teens growth in prescriptions filled.
  - Better margins alongside lower member prices.
- **Gas.** AI tools for gas inventory and sales were announced in December 2025. No later update was found (Unverified).
- **Demand sensing.** Costco's analytics VP describes a loop connecting data, inventory, demand and pricing at the local level.
- **Bakery forecasting.** A reported $100M saving from 2018 appears only in secondary sources (Unverified).
- **Capital spending.** About $7.5B planned for FY27 (up from $6.4B), with heavy supply-chain spending.

- **Digital gap.** Online sales are about 7% of Costco's net sales, versus more than 27% at Sam's Club.
- **Retreats and friction.** Costco Next was discontinued, checkout lines are still criticized, and pre-scan draws mixed reactions.

- **AI risks.** The 10-K now flags formal AI risks.

- **Pharmacy and fuel templates (P1, P2).** Costco's autonomous pharmacy reordering is a concrete benchmark for Walmart and Sam's Club.
- **Low-hype adoption.** Expect a fast follower whose AI wins arrive quietly and keep member trust (Assumption).

## 8. Kroger: detailed profile

### 8.1 Summary
- **New leadership tied AI to the turnaround.**
  - Greg Foran (formerly at Walmart) became CEO on 9 February 2026.
  - The October 2026 Investor Day plan includes a "Modernize How We Operate" pillar, with technology and AI as enablers.
- **AI ownership consolidated.**
  - Milen Mahadevan, CEO of 84.51° (Kroger's analytics unit), became Kroger's first Chief Data and AI Officer (March 2026).
  - He reports to Yael Cosset, EVP and Chief Digital Officer.
- **Most mature GenAI is customer-facing.** The AI Shopping Assistant is live on 100% of banners, and repeat users spend 11% more.
- **Associate AI at scale.** Sage (built with IBM) has 95+ use cases and up to 150,000 users.
- **Biggest setback: robotic fulfillment.** Kroger closed three Ocado automated fulfillment centers and took about $2.5B in charges.
- **Self-assessment.** The CFO says Kroger is at the early stages.

### 8.2 Strategy, partners and investment
- **Framework.** Kroger describes three stages: assisted, augmented and autonomous. How much autonomy a task gets depends on its risk, how repetitive it is and how much human judgment it needs. The long-term vision is "grocery as a managed service".
- **Partners:**
  - **Google Cloud:** Gemini Enterprise for Customer Experience runs the shopping assistant (January 2026).
  - **Instacart:** powers the agentic shopping experience and is the main delivery partner.
  - **IBM:** helped build Sage.
  - **84.51°:** in-house data science and retail media.
  - Microsoft or OpenAI GenAI partnerships: Unverified.
- **Investment.**
  - No AI dollar figure has been disclosed.
  - Kroger targets about $4B in annual savings by 2029 and about $5B reinvested in lower prices.
- **2026 hires.** Chief People Officer Emilee De Martino, Chief eCommerce Officer Nate Faust and Chief Store Operations Officer Mark Ibbotson.

### 8.3 Product content and discovery (P3, P2)
- **AI Shopping Assistant (scaling).**
  - It handles multi-step tasks such as building large carts, reordering and comparing products.
  - It turns recipes into carts and bases suggestions on Kroger's actual stock and prices.
  - Shoppers can photograph handwritten lists to build a cart.
  - It launched 28 July 2026 and reached 100% of banners by 20 October, with an 11% spend uplift for repeat users.
- **GenAI catalog content.** None disclosed (Unverified).
- **Retail media (Kroger Precision Marketing).**
  - Profit grew 24% in Q2 2026 in its best quarter since 2021.

### 8.4 Customer service automation (P3, P5)

- **Delivery.** Delivery service runs largely through Instacart (Assumption: Instacart handles some delivery-related contacts).
- **Online sign-up friction.** Only about 40% of people who start online sign-up finish it.
- **Returns.** No GenAI returns automation disclosed.

### 8.5 Store operations (P5, P1, P2)
- **Sage.** It has 95+ use cases and up to 150,000 users, including checking schedules and requesting time off. It was partly credited for record retention in 2025.
- **Next steps.**
  - Agents that filter dozens of daily notifications and tasks for frontline teams.
  - AI literacy training for 400,000+ associates.
- **Computer vision.**
  - The "Tally" shelf-scanning robot was piloted.
  - Computer-vision data is shared with suppliers.
  - New "Fresh" formats include in-store AI and digital shelf labels.
- **Outcomes.** On-shelf availability hit an all-time high in Q2 2026, though not attributed to AI alone.
- **Shrink.** It improved in 2025 with AI-enabled tools, but worsened in Q2 2026 in fresh food.
- **Labor scheduling.** No AI schedule optimization disclosed (Unverified).

### 8.6 Inventory allocation and supply chain (P1, P4, P2)
- **Forecasting.** Mature machine-learning demand forecasting through 84.51° (not GenAI).
- **AI exception tool and RFID.**
  - The exception tool connects 20 data feeds and shows each item in each store.
  - The bakery RFID pilot (~1,500 stores) reads 98% of tags, counts about 70% faster and cut waste by 50%.
  - RFID is planned for 2,300+ stores by 2029.
- **Ocado retreat.**
  - Kroger closed three automated fulfillment centers and canceled a planned site, taking about $2.5B in charges.
  - Five automated sites remain.
  - It shifted to fulfilling from stores plus Instacart, DoorDash and Uber.
- **Delivery.**
  - 60% of store-based deliveries arrive in under two hours, and 23% in under one hour.
  - Kroger targets $500M of extra online profit (EBITDA, excluding media) by 2029.
- **Network and sourcing.** An automated distribution center opens in Kentucky in 2028, and direct sourcing will rise from under 1% to more than 10%.

- **Weak sales.** Q2 identical sales grew only 0.2%, and 2026 guidance was lowered.

### 8.9 Implications for Walmart

- **Fresh-food execution (P1, P5).** RFID, computer vision shared with suppliers and the AI exception tool target fresh quality and in-stocks.
- **Lesson from Ocado (P4).** Automation must match order density, which supports Walmart's store-as-fulfillment-node model.

## 9. Alibaba Group (commerce): detailed profile

### 9.1 Summary
- **Most aggressive full-stack AI bet in commerce.**
  - Alibaba builds its own Qwen models, T-Head chips and cloud.
  - About RMB 190B of its RMB 380B AI capex plan was spent by 30 June 2026, and management expects to exceed the plan.
- **Reorganized around agentic commerce.**
  - China e-commerce, Alibaba International and Freshippo were merged into Alibaba E-commerce Group.
  - CEO Eddie Wu leads the new "Alibaba Token Hub" AI group.
- **Agentic shopping at national scale.**
  - In May 2026 the Qwen app was connected to Taobao's full catalog, and 250M users have tried AI-driven shopping.
  - No spend or retention data has been published.
- **Seller-side productivity is the hardest evidence.**
  - Dianxiaomi, the merchant customer-service AI, lifted conversion 30% across 200,000 merchants.
  - Accio, the B2B sourcing agent, has 10M+ monthly users.
- **Risk.** Merchant ad revenue fell 7%, and the AI unit lost RMB 13.9B in a quarter.

### 9.2 Strategy and investment
- **Owners.**
  - Eddie Wu, CEO. Joe Tsai, Chairman.
  - Jiang Fan, CEO of Alibaba E-commerce Group.
  - Hema CEO Yan Xiaolei now reports to Jiang Fan.
- **Build, not buy.** Qwen is refined on more than 20 years of Alibaba transaction data.
- **Capex.**
  - Capex rose 75% in the June quarter. Wu expects AI capex to break even in about three years.
  - The timeframe of the RMB 380B plan is reported inconsistently (2026–29 per Reuters, versus three years from February 2025 per management).
- **Hardware.** Quark AI glasses connect to Taobao, Alipay and Amap. The Qwen Glasses N1 Pro (October 2026) adds hands-free payment.

### 9.3 Product content and discovery (P3, P2)
- **Qwen app and Taobao assistant (at scale).**
  - It covers 4B+ products, with agent skills for orders, logistics and after-sales.
  - Features include AI virtual try-on, coupon optimization and automatic buying at a target price.
- **AI search.**
  - Taobao search runs on Qwen and handles long-tail requests.
  - Broker data show an 11.3% click-through rate on AI summaries (secondary source).
- **Merchant content.**
  - AI tools have saved 5M merchants a cumulative RMB 100B+ (company claim).
  - Tmall's new-product innovation center reports 20x faster product development.
  - Qwen-Image 3.1, aimed at e-commerce visuals, is due later in 2026.
- **International and B2B.**
  - AliExpress's operations agent translates listings and chooses logistics routes across 200+ markets (low confidence).
  - Accio has 10M+ monthly users, and Accio Work has 50,000+ paying merchants.

### 9.4 Customer service automation (P2, P3)
- **Dianxiaomi.** 30% higher conversion across 200,000 pilot merchants. It is being upgraded to hold a conversation and complete tasks in one session.
- **After-sales.**
  - Order-retention tools recovered RMB 840M+ in refunds.
  - An after-sales AI assistant launches in 2026.
- **GenAI refund fraud.**
  - Buyers are using GenAI to fake product defects.
  - Taobao/Tmall runs a fake-image detection model in disputes and refunds, first for merchants rated 4.8 or above.
  - Its account-integrity system has recovered RMB 4B+ for merchants.

### 9.5 Store operations: Freshippo/Hema (P1, P5, P2)
- **AI ordering (company claim, single source, no baseline).**
  - Product mix is tuned to shopper preferences within 3 km of each store, changing 30% of the category mix.
  - Store teams accept 96% of AI ordering suggestions.
  - Stockouts are down 25% and waste down 30%.
- **Formats.**
  - Hema scaled back membership stores and is focusing on Freshippo Fresh and NB discount stores.
  - FY2026 sales (GMV) topped RMB 107B, the second straight year of positive adjusted EBITA.
- **Front warehouses.** Delivery-only warehouses are expanding, though customers report fewer products and more stockouts.
- **Associate tools and AI scheduling.** None disclosed (Unverified).

### 9.6 Inventory allocation and supply chain (P4, P1)
- **Quick commerce.**
  - Taobao Instant Commerce revenue rose 45% to RMB 53.3B.
  - It is expected to turn profitable in FY2029.
  - Hema is being integrated with Taobao's front-warehouse network.
- **Cainiao (Alibaba's logistics arm).**
  - The ZeeBot rack-climbing robot doubles storage and retrieval productivity, with 100+ units running in Dongguan.
  - Global 3-Day Delivery launched 5 August 2026.
  - Cainiao's revenue declined in the March quarter.
- **AliExpress.** Reached operating profit in the June quarter.

### 9.7 Alibaba maturity scorecard
| Area | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Product content and discovery | 5 | Agentic shopping across 4B+ products; return on investment unproven |
| Customer service | 4 | Merchant service agents scaling; fraud detection live but new |
| Store operations (Hema) | 3 | Strong AI ordering claims from a single source; store footprint changing |
| Inventory and supply chain | 4 | AI forecasting in fresh, AI-run robotics, three-day cross-border delivery; quick commerce unprofitable |

### 9.8 Setbacks and risks
- **Profit.** Quarterly profit fell 75% as capex rose 75%. The prior quarter brought the first operating loss since 2021.
- **Monetization gap.** Agentic investment is not yet offsetting erosion in the ad business (analyst view).
- **Delivery subsidy war.** It cost the industry RMB 100B+ in 2025 (low-to-medium confidence source).
- **Geopolitics.** The Pentagon added Alibaba to its Section 1260H list of Chinese military companies in June 2026; Alibaba denies military ties. Tariffs are weighing on international e-commerce.
- **Compute costs.** A new server costs about twice what it did a year earlier.

### 9.9 Implications for Walmart
- **Prepare for agent-led shopping (P3, P2).** Make the catalog readable by AI agents, and build ad formats that work inside agent-led shopping before those flows eat into sponsored search.
- **Prioritize agents for sellers (P3).** Dianxiaomi's 30% lift is the benchmark for agents serving Walmart Marketplace sellers.
- **GenAI refund fraud (P2).** This is an immediate threat to any refund policy based on customer photos. Taobao's combination of detection and account integrity is a blueprint.
- **Sam's Club China (P4, P1).** Hema raises the bar on speed and waste. Using Alibaba technology now carries US policy risk (Assumption).
